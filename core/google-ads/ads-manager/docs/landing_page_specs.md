## General Quality Score Guidelines

### Technical Requirements (All Pages)

- **HTTPS:** SSL certificate mandatory
- **Mobile-First:** Responsive design, touch-friendly CTAs
- **Core Web Vitals:**
  - LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): < 2.5s
  - FID (First Input Delay): < 100ms
  - CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): < 0.1
- **Page Speed:** Total load time < 3 seconds
- **Accessibility:** Alt text on images, proper heading hierarchy

### Content Requirements

- **Keyword Placement:** H1, first paragraph, subheadings
- **Local Signals:** City name, areas served, local phone number
- **Trust Signals:** Reviews, accreditations, years in business
- **Clear CTAs:** Above fold, repeated throughout page
- **Unique Content:** No duplicate content from other pages

### Conversion Elements

- Phone number in header (click-to-call on mobile)
- Contact form above fold or easily accessible
- Trust badges near CTAs
- Testimonials/reviews with star ratings
- Clear service descriptions matching ad groups
